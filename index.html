NASA data & resources

Sun and Earth positions come only from JPL Horizons, terrain only from NASA LOLA elevation data, and imagery only from Moon Trek. Site coordinates are approximate published landing-site values: check them in Quickmap. The chips show which source is actually loaded right now.

JPL Horizons

Sun and Earth azimuth and elevation for a Moon-fixed observer (libration included), requested live from the Horizons API for every site.

Open Horizons

LOLA topography

Terrain horizon masks ray-marched across the LRO LOLA south-polar elevation model (it stops at 80°S, so sites further north have no mask). They are built by tools/make_lola_horizons.py and loaded from lola_horizons.json.
